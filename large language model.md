A large language model is a type of artificial intelligence trained on massive amounts of text to understand, summarize, translate, and generate human-like language.

## How It Works
* **Transformers:** They use a neural network design called a transformer that reads entire sentences at the same time rather than word-by-word.
* **Prediction:** They act as giant statistical prediction machines that figure out the most likely next word or token in a sequence.
* **Parameters:** The word "large" refers to the billions of internal variables and weights—called parameters—that help the model capture deep context and grammar.
