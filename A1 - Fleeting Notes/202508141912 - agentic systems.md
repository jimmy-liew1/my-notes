**Agentic systems** represent a significant advancement in [[artificial intelligence|Artificial Intelligence (AI)]], characterized by their ability to plan and execute complex tasks. Unlike earlier AI models, these systems are designed with a higher degree of autonomy and sophistication.

The emergence of agentic systems, anticipated in 2025, is expected to dramatically accelerate the adoption of AI across various sectors. This shift suggests a move towards more capable and versatile AI solutions.
