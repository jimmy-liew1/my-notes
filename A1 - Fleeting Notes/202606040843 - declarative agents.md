### What are Declarative Agents?
Microsoft uses the term "Declarative Agent" for agents that have three core components:
1. **Instructions:** Define the agent's role, scope, boundaries, and response format to shape its behavior and output.
2. **Knowledge Sources:** Curated, trusted data sources (SharePoint, files, URLs) that ground the agent's responses in facts.
3. **Actions (optional):** Extend the agent with connectors and plugins to interact with external systems and services.

Declarative agents uses **generative AI**, but within boundaries you declare - scope, sources, and guardrails.[^1]

### Opportunity Map: Where Declarative Agents Win First
- **HR:** Policy Q&A, Job description bias scan, onboarding FAQ
- **Sales:** Battlecards, pricing guardrails, RFP drafts
- **IT:** How-to troubleshooting from Knowledge Base
- **Finance:** Expense/travel policy interpretation
- **PMO/Delivery:** Project status synthesis across tools
