While Quartz doesn't use `status` to decide _how_ to process a file, many people use it to display the "maturity" of a note on the web page.

If you want to align with the most common Digital Garden/Quartz patterns, you might use these values for your `status` property:
- `status: seedling` (Rough notes)
- `status: budding` (Developing)
- `status: evergreen` (Polished/Finished)
