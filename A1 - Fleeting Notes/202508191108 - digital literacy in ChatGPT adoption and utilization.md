- It's essential for both policymakers and technology developers to understand the factors driving their mainstream adoption.
- This research found that:
	- High digital literacy level correlate with using ChatGPT for testing, entertainment, and acquiring new knowledge.
	- No significant relationship was found between digital literacy and using ChatGPT for work or educational purposes.
- This research suggested that:
	- As digital literacy improves, the use of AI tools like ChatGPT will diversity across different context.
	- The need for targeted digital literacy programs to enhance AI adoption, reduce digital inequalities, and ensure that advanced technologies are accessible to all population segments.
