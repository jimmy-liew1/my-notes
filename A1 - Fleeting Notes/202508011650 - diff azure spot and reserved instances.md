## What is the difference between Azure Spot and reserved instances?
**[[reserved instances|Reserved Instances (RIs)]]** are a key pricing strategy offered by cloud providers like AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud to help businesses save money on compute resources. While [[azure spot instance|Spot Instances]] offer flexibility and deep discounts for temporary workloads, Reserved Instances are all about stability and strategic commitment.
