Digital citizenship refers to the responsible, ethical, and safe use of technology. It encompasses a range of behaviors and practices that enable individuals to participate effectively and positively in online communities and the digital world.

Key aspects of digital citizenship include:
*   **[[digital literacy|Digital Literacy]]:** Understanding how to use technology effectively, critically evaluate online information, and navigate digital environments.
*   **Digital Etiquette (Netiquette):** Adhering to social norms and respectful communication in online interactions.
*   **Digital Law:** Understanding the legal implications of online activities, such as copyright, privacy, and cybercrime.
*   **Digital Rights and Responsibilities:** Recognizing and exercising rights online while also understanding the responsibilities that come with them.
*   **Digital Health and Wellness:** Promoting physical and psychological well-being in relation to technology use, including managing screen time and avoiding digital addiction.
*   **Digital Security:** Protecting oneself and one's data from online threats, such as malware, phishing, and identity theft.
*   **Digital Commerce:** Engaging in online transactions safely and ethically.
*   **Digital Access:** Ensuring equitable access to technology and the internet for all.

In essence, digital citizenship is about being a good digital citizen, contributing to a positive and safe online environment for everyone.
