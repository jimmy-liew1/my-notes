## What Does an Agile Coach Do?
An Agile Coach is a professional who guides organizations in adopting and scaling [[Agile]] practices. Their role goes beyond just mentoring individuals; they focus on fostering a culture of continuous improvement.  

Here's a breakdown of the key responsibilities of an Agile Coach:  
- **Guiding Teams**: Agile Coaches work directly with teams to adopt and implement frameworks like [[Scrum]], [[Kanban]], or [[SAFe]].  
- **Facilitating Change**: Agile Coaches help leadership teams adopt Agile practices, align with business goals, and navigate cultural shifts.  
- **Training and Workshops**: Agile Coaches run training sessions to educate teams on Agile practices and improve collaboration.  
- **Improving Organizational Agility**: They promote a growth mindset and implement strategies to address challenges in the Agile transformation.  
- **Assessing and Measuring Progress**: Agile Coaches track Agile transformation progress and use metrics to drive improvements and celebrate wins.  
  
An Agile Coach is a mentor and facilitator who helps organizations adopt and improve Agile practices. While technical knowledge can be beneficial, it's not a strict requirement.
