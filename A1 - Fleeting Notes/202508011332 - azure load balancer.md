**Azure Load Balancer** is a service that distributes incoming traffic across healthy virtual machines or other services in Azure. It enhances the availability and scalability of applications by providing:
- **High Availability:** By distributing traffic across multiple instances, it ensures that your application remains available even if one instance fails.
- **Scalability:** It can handle increased traffic loads by distributing the load across more instances.
- **Health Probes:** It monitors the health of the instances and only sends traffic to healthy ones.
