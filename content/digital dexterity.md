**Digital dexterity** is defined as the ability and desire to learn and use existing and emerging technologies for better business outcomes.[^1]  It also refers to individual or group's ability to adapt and adopt existing and emerging technologies in their field to produce better results for their company. As the digital world is constantly changing, the desire to adapt and innovate using new technologies is vital for employees.[^2]

It goes beyond basic [[digital literacy]] (knowing how to use specific tools) and encompasses a mindset of continuous learning, experimentation, and strategic application of technology in a rapid changing digital landscape.

## Why Digital Dexterity Matters
In the context of [[digital transformation|Digital Transformation]], digital dexterity is crucial for both individuals and organizations because:
* **Enables Adaptation:** Allows employees and businesses to quickly adopt new tools and processes as technology evolves. 
* **Drives Innovation:** Empowers individuals to identify opportunities to use technology in novel ways to solve problems or create new value. 
* **Improves Productivity:** Effective use of technology leads to more efficient workflows and better results. 
* **Enhances Customer Experience:** Digitally dexterous employees can better utilize tools to interact with and serve customers. 
* **Fosters Resilience:** Organizations with high digital dexterity are better equipped to navigate technological disruptions and market changes. 
* **Supports Continuous Learning:** Cultivates a culture where learning and skill development are ongoing.

## Components and Skills 
Digital dexterity is not a single skill but a combination of abilities and attitudes, including: 
* **Digital Literacy:** Foundational knowledge of how to use common digital tools and platforms. 
* **Technological Fluency:** The ability to understand how different technologies work together and their potential applications. 
* **Continuous Learning Mindset:** A willingness and desire to constantly learn about new technologies. 
* **Adaptability:** The capacity to quickly adjust to new tools, interfaces, and workflows. 
* **Problem-Solving:** Using technology creatively to solve business challenges. 
* **Collaboration:** Utilizing digital tools effectively to work with others. 
* **[[critical thinking|Critical Thinking]]:** Evaluating the suitability and impact of different technologies. 
* **Data Literacy:** Understanding how to access, interpret, and use data from digital sources. 
## Developing Digital Dexterity 
Organizations and individuals can foster digital dexterity through: 
* Providing access to training and resources on new technologies. 
* Encouraging experimentation and safe-to-fail environments. 
* Promoting a culture of continuous learning and knowledge sharing. 
* Providing the right tools and infrastructure. 
* Setting clear expectations around technology adoption and use.
