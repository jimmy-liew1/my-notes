**Digital literacy** encompasses the skills and knowledge needed to use digital devices, communication applications, and networks effectively. It involves understanding how to find, evaluate, use, share, and create content using various digital technologies. Key aspects include information literacy, media literacy, and the ability to use technology safely and responsibly.
