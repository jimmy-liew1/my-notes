Scrum is a specific framework within the [[agile|Agile]] umbrella. It provides structured roles (like [[scrum master|Scrum Master]], [[product owner|Product Owner]], and [[development team|Development Team]]), defined ceremonies (like [[daily stand-ups|Daily Stand-ups]], [[sprint planning|Sprint planning]], and [[sprint retrospective|Retrospectives]]), and uses time-boxed iterations (called [[scrum sprint|Sprints]]). Scrum is one of the most popular ways to implement Agile, but it is not synonymous with Agile itself.
