**Retrieval-augmented Generation (RAG)** is a technique that enhances the capabilities of [[LLM|large language models (LLMs)]] by augmenting their knowledge base with external information. Instead of relying solely on the data they were trained on, RAG systems first retrieve relevant information from a knowledge source (like a database or a collection of documents) and then use this retrieved information to inform the LLM's response generation. This allows LLMs to provide more accurate, up-to-date, and contextually relevant answers.
