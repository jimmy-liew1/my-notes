**Social engineering** involves manipulating people through interaction and emotions to gain unauthorized access. Victims are tricked into revealing sensitive information or compromising security measures.

A social engineering attack usually involves several steps. First, the attacker researches the victim to gather useful information. Then, they build trust with the target before tricking them into sharing sensitive data or breaking security rules.

The effectiveness of social engineering lies in exploiting natural human tendencies like trust, helpfulness, fear, or curiosity. Defending against it requires awareness, skepticism, and strong security protocols that include human training.
