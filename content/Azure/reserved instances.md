**Reserved Instances (RIs)**, in AWS and Azure, are a pricing model that offers significant discounts (up to 72% or slightly more, up to 75% in some scenarios) compared to On-Demand or pay-as-you-go pricing rates. These savings are achieved by committing to a specific instance configuration for a 1-year or 3-year term.[^1][^2][^3][^4] They are designed for stable, predictable workloads that run continuously, allowing for better budget forecasting and reduced operational costs.
